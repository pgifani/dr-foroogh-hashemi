About the doctor

A pediatrician who treats the whole family, not just the chart.

Dr. Hashemi has spent more than eighteen years helping families raise healthy, happy kids. She believes good pediatric care is a partnership: clear explanations, honest answers, and enough time to actually talk. From first checkups to teenage years, she is a steady hand parents can count on.

  • Board-certified in Pediatrics, American Board of Pediatrics
  • Experienced with newborns — gentle care from day one
  • Bilingual visits available in English and Farsi

Good questions

Answers before you book.

Still unsure about something? Ask the care assistant in the corner, or call us any weekday.

Yes — we're welcoming new patients from newborns through age 18, and we accept most major insurance plans. Bring your card to your first visit and we'll handle the billing. Not sure about your plan? Call us and we'll check for you.

Please bring your insurance card, a photo ID, your child's immunization records if you have them, and a list of any medications or allergies. Arriving 10 minutes early gives us time for the paperwork.

We keep same-week slots open for sick visits. Request a time through the form or call us early in the day for the best availability. If this is a medical emergency, call 115.

Ask your pharmacy to send a refill request, or call our office. Some medications need a quick check-in first, so please allow up to two business days.

For anything severe — trouble breathing, a seizure, or a serious injury — call 115 right away. For other questions, contact the clinic during working hours or book a visit here on the site.
