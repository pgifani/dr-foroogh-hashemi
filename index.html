About the doctor

A pediatrician who treats the whole family, not just the chart.

Dr. Hashemi has spent more than fifteen years helping families raise healthy, happy kids. She believes good pediatric care is a partnership: clear explanations, honest answers, and enough time to actually talk. From first checkups to teenage years, she is a steady hand parents can count on.

  • Board-certified in Pediatrics, American Board of Pediatrics
  • Newborn & NICU-experienced — gentle care from day one
  • Bilingual visits available in English and Farsi

Care for every age

From first breaths to first jobs.

Comprehensive pediatric care under one roof, so your family sees a doctor who already knows your story.

Newborn & first days

A complete newborn exam in the first days of life, including jaundice assessment and treatment, with reassurance for every new-parent concern.

Breastfeeding & nutrition

Breastfeeding guidance from pregnancy through birth, nutrition counseling for nursing mothers, and newborn-care coaching.

Growth & development

Regular health and development monitoring from birth through the end of puberty (age 18).

Infections & digestion

Infectious diseases and GI issues: acute and chronic diarrhea and constipation, abdominal pain, colic, and reflux.

Kidney & urinary

Urinary tract infections, stones, and bedwetting.

Asthma & allergies

Asthma and skin, eczema, respiratory, and digestive allergies.

Respiratory & ear

Lung and airway infections (croup and bronchiolitis) and ear infections.

Puberty & adolescence

Periodic exams, assessment of normal or early puberty, nutrition and hygiene counseling, height-growth strategies, and obesity care.

Ear piercing

In fully sterile conditions with the American Studex system and hypoallergenic 24k-gold-plated earrings — from 2 months of age.
